# Plan for waves 116 to 150 (written 2026-11-20 after owner round 7; replaces the 116-121 part of docs/ROUND6.md)

Owner rules for this run: ONE WAVE AT A TIME (each wave ends with tests, a push to main and an Android build; then stop, report, and wait for the owner's go);
use the tokens it needs; never lose work (backup snapshots, agent notes, resumable script). Agent effort (owner's option 1): builders, testers, integrator and
CI agent at HIGH, the independent review of each wave at MAX. Every UI check is driven by real finger events (docs/ROUND7.md rule).
Owner round 7 words are in docs/ROUND7.md. Script: docs/sprint/wave_runner.js (one wave per launch, args {"wave": N, "skipTags": [...], ...}).

118 TANKS AND HELICOPTERS you can find: fixed places in the world, map icons, demo SPAWN list, call-in; enter, drive, fly and shoot by touch.
119 SNAKE BLASTS: snake species that burst in their own ways (their own attacks, apart from the gun arsenal), readable looks and radius rings.
120 WHOLE MAP REACHABLE: no invisible walls; walk, swim, climb or fly to any mountain, river, shore or rooftop; nothing to fall through.
121 BUILDINGS WITH FLOORS: walk in from the street, stairs, lifts, upper floors, rooftops.
122 BUILDING DETAIL: rooms that match the outside, furniture, windows you can see through, shops.
123 STREET DETAIL: sidewalk clutter, storefront depth, signs, street life.
124 FRAME RATE: measured recovery on the phone tiers (owner: 37 to 45 FPS on LOW+, drops to 13 to 28 in flight); win back draw, triangle and script headroom.
