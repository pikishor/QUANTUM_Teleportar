# World expansion plan (owner request 2026-11-08, build 231b34d feedback, rating 3.5/10 "better than before, lots of work")

Owner wants: a huge world: mountains outside the city, ocean, rivers, bridges; airport with planes and helicopters (flyable), things in the
mountains too; more aliens and better enemies; more destructive weapons including Halo-style energy weapons (generic names, original designs);
a much better, more detailed UI for menus, options and weapon sections; better story plus free roam; animals in mountains and outskirts;
more detailed cars, streets, houses and buildings.

## Phone feasibility rules (non-negotiable)
Terrain = heightmap chunks streamed around the player with 3 LODs, fog and far impostors; ocean = one shader plane; rivers = flow-shader strips;
vegetation and rocks = MultiMesh by distance; animals = pooled instanced critters near the player; LOW/MED/HIGH tiers scale distance and density.
Perf gate and texture limits still apply (LOW 200 / MED 260 / HIGH 360 MB). World scale target: 4-6 km square, city 1 km inside.

## Sprint order
104 terrain, biomes, ocean, rivers, roads to outskirts | 105 bridges, harbour, boats, mountain sites, vegetation | 106 airport, planes, helicopters,
air traffic and flight controls | 107 animals and alien wildlife by biome | 108 energy weapons set, destructive weapons, enemy roster expansion
| 109 UI overhaul for menus, options, weapon sections | 110 story acts and free-roam content | 111 car, street and building detail pass | 112 director review + fixes.

## Art sources
CC0: Quaternius (Ultimate Animals, nature, vehicles, aircraft packs when available), OpenGameArt, ambientCG/Poly Haven (terrain textures, HDRIs).
Higher-detail cars/buildings may need Sketchfab (CC0/CC-BY with attribution; needs an API token from the owner) or purchased packs.

## Owner additions (2026-11-08, later)
- ALL weapon categories: COD-type weapons, Halo-inspired energy weapons, tanks and heavy vehicles; inspired designs get ORIGINAL names with similar power and capabilities (no trademarked names).
- Railways (trains on tracks, stations, rideable, train missions) and ships (cargo, ferries, cruise, naval vessels).
- Mountains with valleys and roads to reach them; casual 'time-pass' play anywhere, with enemies and wildlife still finding the player.
- Buildings: reach rooftops through stairs and lifts, snipe with zoom, fire energy weapons from roofs; blast radius and effects improved; HERO MODE unlocks everything.
- Better animation for humans, cars and every entity; open-source assets plus our own design on top.
Sprint script: world104.js (waves 104-114: terrain/water, bridges/harbour/rail/ships/outskirts, airport/aircraft, animals/aliens, weapons incl. tanks/enemies,
UI overhaul, story/free roam, car/street/building detail, vertical buildings/casual play/hero mode/blast table, animation overhaul, director review).

## Status (wave 104, terrain foundation done; details in godot/ARCHITECTURE.md "Terrain world")

## Status (wave 105, bridges, harbour and boats; details in godot/ARCHITECTURE.md "Bridges, harbour and boats")
Every road crossing of a river or lake is a real bridge now (six road/track bridges, deck 3.6 m over the water, river bed left alone, collision, cars ride the deck), plus footbridges over EASTWATER and NORTH CREEK, the HIGHLINE rail trestle (two tracks, ready for the railway wave) and the HARBOUR GATE suspension bridge (630 m, towers 55 m, traffic crossing). The harbour on the south coast (apron, dredged basin, three piers, 238 containers, warehouses, three gantry cranes, tank farm, ferry terminal, lighthouse, the FERRY sailing a loop) is carved into the terrain; a HARBOUR ROAD links it to the COAST ROAD. Boats: speedboat, JET SKI, PATROL BOAT float on the real waves (buoyancy, planing, wake, bow wave, run aground at shores and quays), six berths at the piers, the hero steps out onto a pier, BOAT RUN + dock hooks (`BoatRun.hooks()`) for boat missions. Next: 106 airport, planes, helicopters.

## Status (wave 106, railways and ships; details in godot/ARCHITECTURE.md "Railways and ships")
8 km of double-track railway in three lines: the MAGLEV on a viaduct over the whole city (five stations, CENTRAL on the street), the MOUNTAIN LINE (overpass, trestle over NORTH CREEK, a long viaduct, a 346 m tunnel to an underground terminus) and the COAST LINE (a level crossing over the FOREST ROAD, HARBOUR STATION). Six trains run on timetables (maglev, express, ore, regional, container): board them, stand and walk inside (the moving car carries the hero), sit, climb to the roof, jump off; level crossings with barriers, lamps and a bell; signals; horns; four train missions (heist, escort, derail, roof chase). Ships: a boardable, drivable NAVAL DESTROYER (helm, two gun turrets with shells, helipad), a CONTAINER SHIP, a SUBMARINE (surfaced), a cruise liner, a tanker and a freighter sailing the deep south, channel buoys and a lighthouse beam. Next in the sprint order stays as planned (animals, weapons, UI).

## Status (wave 109b, HUD and in-game UI for the big world; details in godot/ARCHITECTURE.md "HUD and in-game UI for the big world")
The game's own screens now know the world. Outside the city, in the air or on the water a landmark COMPASS (airport, harbour, sites, the goal, ENTERING <place>) sits in the objective stack with a route chip ("4.9 KM  ETA 4:06  CAR"); the GPS has three modes (car over the real highways and tracks, air in a straight line, boat over the water) shown on the minimap and on the map, where waypoints can now be placed anywhere in the world, the zoom has REGION and WORLD levels with biome and river names, a colour key and badges for the airport, the harbour, the helipads and the sites; the minimap pins the landmarks and widens per vehicle; every vehicle family has its readout (hull row with SOUND / DAMAGED / SMOKING / BURNING, nitro, the boat's depth and shallow warning, the tank's turret dial and reload ring, the helicopter's rotor, the plane's stall margin); health bars keep a ghost of what was lost and flash on a hit; the wanted stars say SPOTTED / SEARCHING / HIDDEN and what the police send; two small feeds list kills and pickups (pickup toasts moved there). The default frame stays within 12 controls.
