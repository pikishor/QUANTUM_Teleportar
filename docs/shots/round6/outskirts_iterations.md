# Outskirts towns and the 9 km world: render-look-fix loops (wave 112, builder w112-outskirts-map)

All frames: software Vulkan (xvfb), phone aspect 2400x1080 for the final passes, MED and LOW, real game scene, looked at as a phone player. Scratch frames were deleted after the sheets.
Sheets in this folder: `outskirts_before_after.jpg`, `outskirts_towns_aerial_med.jpg`, `outskirts_streets_med.jpg`, `outskirts_low_and_night.jpg`, `outskirts_interiors.jpg`, `outskirts_world_and_plans.jpg`.
Reference: `docs/owner_shots/round5` (the owner's frames: empty outskirts, a long dark road, cuboid hedges, a bare lilac shore, a flat map with a few site pins).

| loop | what was rendered | concrete defects seen | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | standalone viewer, farm / industrial / suburb / harbour on a flat pad | trees and cones were back faces (invisible from outside); every colour washed out (vertex colours were read as linear); awnings blotchy | winding of cones and blobs, `pow(COLOR, 2.2)` in the shader, awning stripe code |
| 2 | viewer, street and house views | brick walls shimmered as noise beyond 20 m; terraces counted as one house, the home target was overshot | patterns fade to the average colour (`detail_near` / `detail_far`), homes counted per unit with a cap |
| 3 | viewer, industrial works and harbour | stack and crane stood outside the flat pad; boats and the lighthouse did not fit | landmarks slide into the pad (`SettlementPlan._nudge`), ground patches are clamped to the ellipse |
| 4 | real game MED, one aerial and one street frame per town (7 towns) | SALT QUAY faced away from the sea (the quay was on the land side); lawns a saturated green against the olive land; HIGH LARK chalets nearly black | the harbour reads the water direction (`SettlementPlan.sea`) and lays quay, lighthouse, crane and boats towards it; lawns blend to the land colour; brighter log and roof palettes (and, from the anchor check: church, water tower and mast for the suburb, a mast for the resort, a water tower for the lodge) |
| 5 | folk close-ups (3 m) at four towns | every farmer had the same coat and hat; the dog read as a table | per-person seed jitter inside the archetype (`SettlementFolk.seed_of(role, key)`), nose, eyes and saddle patch on the dog |
| 6 | night, BAYVIEW and SUNSET COVE | windows glow but the street is dark between the lamps; no defect that blocks play | none needed (the lamps are lit, the window glow is the shader's `night` uniform) |
| 7 | world map at the phone resolution | the town names overlapped the region names ("HIGH LARK" on "FOREST HILLS", "SALT QUAY" on "SILVER MERE") | the town names reserve their boxes first, region names step aside (`MapScreen._draw_world_labels`) |
| 8 | LOW street views with the draw count of every frame, A/B with `QT_NO_TOWNS=1` and `QT_TOWNS_AB` | +6 draws and +28 k primitives in a LOW street (chunks at 260 m, shadows on every chunk) | LOW near range 190 m (chunks built within 165 m), shadows only on chunks within 150 m (MED) / 210 m (HIGH), far meshes hidden inside the city |
| 9 | final phone pass: 7 towns at MED (aerial + street), 7 at LOW, interiors, map | brick still too busy at 8 m (7 cm rows alias on a phone); a hay bale in the middle of the lodge road; the plans (`tests/dev_plan_png.gd`) showed the harbour with 18 homes and lanes that lead to no house | coarser brick (10 cm rows) fading out by 22 m; bales stay off the road; quay terraces of 1 - 3 units and a 42 % / 36 % row budget (`budget`, 69 homes); empty lanes are pruned |

Judged against the round 5 frames: the outskirts now have places (windows, roofs, porches, fences, lamps, mailboxes, hedges, palms, parked cars, people at their posts), each town has its own colours and silhouette (red-roofed clapboard farm, brick works with stack and crane, pastel terraces on the quay, white villas with palms, dark log chalets, flat-roofed adobe with containers), the map shows named town pins without clutter, and the interiors are warm and lit instead of the dark neon boxes of the city types.
Still not good (honest list): the main street through each town is the wide highway profile (28 m of asphalt reads as a runway at HARROW FIELD and SUNSET COVE), the people stand at their posts instead of walking, pets do not move, the harbour has no pier or water traffic, houses of one style are variations of 3 - 5 recipes, and the far view of a town is a box-and-roof silhouette without ground patches.
