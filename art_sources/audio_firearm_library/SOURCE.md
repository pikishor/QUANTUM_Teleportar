# The Free Firearm Sound Library (Prepared SFX Library)

- Author: The Free Firearm Sound Library team (college students, Kickstarter 2013), uploaded by Ylmir
- Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
- Pages (checked in round 4, all show CC0):
  - https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
- Note: CC0 'no rights reserved' statement on the page; 96 kHz 24-bit stereo field recordings of 23 real firearms, near and mid distance.

Only the clips the game uses are kept here (32 clips, 693 KB): cut by tools/audio/real_clips.py (mono, 44.1 kHz, peak -1 dBFS, OGG q6; loops FLAC), turned into the game's sounds by tools/audio/make_real.py. The rest of the pack is not in the repo; download it from the page above.
